Key Insights

Data-driven observations from our collected sample of 47 verified remote technology job listings.

💻

Developer is the Largest Category

In our collected sample, 20 of 47 jobs (42.6%) are Software Developer roles, leading all technical domains.

🎯

Senior Roles Dominate Demand

In our collected sample, 33 of 47 jobs (70.2%) target Senior-level candidates, showing high remote demand for experienced talent.

💵

Salary Disclosure Rate: 38.3%

In our collected sample, 18 of 47 jobs disclose compensation upfront, while 29 roles omit salary details.

🏢

39 Unique Hiring Companies

In our collected sample, hiring is widely distributed across 39 unique employers, indicating diverse market participation.

🌍

United States Leads Geography

In our collected sample, the United States accounts for 24 of 47 listings, making it the leading geographic restriction.
